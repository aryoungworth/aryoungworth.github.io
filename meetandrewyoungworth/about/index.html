Andrew Youngworth is studying Journalism at Syracuse University. He wants to talk about sports on air after college. Andrew is involved with CitrusTV, WAER, z89, and the ACC Network here at Syracuse.

Andrew is from Marshfield Massachusetts, a coastal town right in between Boston and Cape Cod. Andrew graduated from Marshfield High School in 2025. His parents, Allison and Mark, also both attended Syracuse University. Andrew has watched Syracuse athletics since he was little. That was part of his decision when choosing which college to attend.

When Andrew's not in school, he enjoys watching sports, which includes enjoying the Celtics, the Bruins, and Chelsea F.C, and likely being dissapointed by the Falcons, Red Sox, and any Syracuse team. Outside of sports, Andrew loves going to the beach back home in the Summer. He also loves watching tv shows and movies. His favorite artist is Noah Kahan, and his favorite food is chicken parm.
